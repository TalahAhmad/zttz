Upper Body Split

Saved on this device

Two upper days a week built around back, shoulders and arms, with no chest work. Every exercise is 2 working sets in the 12 to 20 rep range, so each session runs about 30 to 35 minutes.

2 upper days / week 6 exercises per day 2 sets each 12–20 reps 60–90 s rest

Sample week

MonLower
TueUpper A
WedRest
ThuLower
FriUpper B
SatRest
SunRest

Slide Upper A and Upper B into whatever days are open around her lower days. Try to keep at least one day between A and B so grip and rear delts recover. If she wants a third upper day, repeat A the following week first, then alternate.

How to run it

Warm upOne light set of the first exercise of the day. It does not count as a working set.
EffortBoth sets should end with only 1 or 2 clean reps left in the tank.
TempoLift in about 1 second, lower in 2 to 3 seconds. No swinging.
Rest60 to 90 seconds between sets and between exercises.
ProgressWhen she hits the top of the rep range on both sets, go up the smallest weight jump next session.
Finish position Start position Direction of the lift
